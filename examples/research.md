# Research files

Copy to `~/.claude/skill-context/research.md`. Skills that record product behaviour append it here through `research-file-update`; without this file they skip that step.

- Research directory: `~/notes/research/`.
- Files: `<product>-usage-research.md` (evidence), `<product>-best-practices.md` (distilled rules), `<product>-intent.md` (ticket status and settled decisions).
